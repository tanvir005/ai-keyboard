Nib.

The AI editor built into your keyboard.

Nib is a custom iOS keyboard that rewrites, fixes tone, and translates what you're typing — before you hit send, without leaving the app you're already in.

Most writing help lives behind a copy-paste round trip: draft in Messages, paste into an AI app, copy the result back. Nib collapses that into one tap, sitting directly above the system keyboard wherever text gets typed — Mail, Messages, Notes, dating apps, Slack.

Who it's for

People who type fast and edit slow: professionals sending client emails on the move, non-native English speakers who want their texts to land right, anyone who's ever stared at a reply for two minutes deciding on tone.

How it works

Select text (or none, for the whole draft) → tap an action in the AI toolbar — Rewrite, Tone, Fix, Translate, Synonyms, Reply, or Ask AI anything → get 2–3 alternatives inline → tap one to swap it in, or tap regenerate for another pass. No screen leaves the keyboard.

Why this scope

Built from a teardown of 10 shipped AI-keyboard apps — TypeAI, KeyAI, Grammarly, Rizzkey, Wispr Flow, and others. The seven tools above are what nearly all of them converged on independently, so we treat them as table stakes. What we left out on purpose — voice dictation, theme/emoji shops, dating-reply generators — are different products wearing a keyboard's clothes. That's a v2 conversation, not this one.

5Allow Full AccessThe #1 fear in every competitor's reviews — named and answered.
9:41
One permission, fully explained

iOS keyboards are sandboxed by default — Full Access is what lets Nib reach the AI at all. Here's exactly what that does and doesn't mean:

Only sent when you tap a toolbar action — never continuously
Only the selected text (or current draft) — not your keystroke history
Never logged after the AI responds — nothing stored on our servers
Revoke anytime — Nib still works as a plain keyboard without it
